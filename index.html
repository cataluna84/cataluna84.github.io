About

Research ideas, built into working systems

I'm the Founder, CEO, and Principal AI Research Engineer at OM Enterprises — AI Research & Engineering Labs. I build robust computer-vision and multimodal systems for data-scarce settings, domain shifts, and real deployment constraints across image, video, 3D, audio, speech, and text.

My work connects research implementation with systems engineering: translating ideas into training and evaluation pipelines, running experiments on accelerators, studying model behavior, and optimizing inference for practical constraints.

I work across multimodal and multilingual models, speech, computer vision, mechanistic interpretability, and model efficiency. Recent work includes low-resource speech translation, cross-lingual safety analysis, language identification across compact models, and vision interpretability.

Research interests include Generative AI (diffusion, GANs, VAEs, and controllable generation), vision-language models, data-efficient learning, model robustness, multilingual LLMs, State Space Models, and how these methods support robotics and autonomous systems.

Over 15+ years I've contributed to 50+ research papers, advising researchers and PhDs on engineering optimizations and bouncing novel ideas to scale research across ICLR, ICML, CVPR, NeurIPS, ECCV and ML Summit. I also mentor, advise and sometimes judge founders at a startup accelerator on efficient engineering, product-market fit and funding.

Applied work spans retail, content creation, media (podcasts, news and publishing), healthcare, gaming (storytelling through immersive voices), avatars and voice agents with localized accents, logistics, recruiting, and sales and support. I've deployed on-device (iOS), on edge (DeepStream SDK) and at scale (CUDA/Triton).

I'm a TWiML Contributor Lead (5+ years) running the weekly Generative AI Problem Solving, Research & Impact meetup, a Cohere Labs Community Lead & Researcher, an MLT Tokyo contributor, and a core organizer of Lucknow AI Labs.
